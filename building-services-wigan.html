FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About Our Building Services

Useful answers about house extensions, new build homes, brickwork, groundwork and working with L&R Design & Build.

What building services do L&R Design & Build provide?

We provide house extensions, new build homes, brickwork, groundwork, renovations, landscaping, fencing, driveways, garden rooms, garage conversions, patios, paving and drainage.

Do you build single-storey and double-storey extensions?

Yes. We build both single-storey and double-storey extensions, from smaller home improvements through to larger house extension projects.

Do you build new build homes?

Yes. Our new build construction work can cover the project from initial groundwork through brickwork and construction to the finished structure.

What brickwork services do you provide?

Brickwork services include walls, extensions, repairs, alterations and brickwork for new build projects.

Do you provide groundwork and drainage?

Yes. Our groundwork services include foundations, excavation, preparation, drainage and ground preparation. Drainage work includes installations, repairs and alterations.

Where do you provide building services?

L&R Design & Build is based in Wigan and works across Greater Manchester and the wider North West, including Leigh, Manchester, Salford, Warrington, Wilmslow, Lymm, Macclesfield, Cheshire, Liverpool and Bolton.
