Frequently Asked Questions

What information should I include when I contact you about a building project?

Tell us what work you are considering, where the property is located, whether you have planning permission or drawings and when you would ideally like the work to begin. A short description of what you want to achieve is also helpful.

Can I contact you if I do not have drawings or planning permission yet?

Yes. You can contact us at an early stage of your project. Let us know whether planning or drawings are approved, in progress, not started or not required.

Do you provide free quotes for building work?

Yes. L&R Design & Build provides free quotes for building projects. Giving us clear information about your project helps us understand what you need.

What types of building projects can I contact you about?

You can contact us about house extensions, new build homes, brickwork, groundwork, renovations, garage conversions, garden rooms, driveways, landscaping, fencing, drainage, patios and paving.

Do you take on building projects outside Wigan?

Yes. We are based in Wigan and work across Greater Manchester and the wider North West, including Leigh, Manchester, Salford, Warrington, Wilmslow, Lymm, Macclesfield, Cheshire, Liverpool and Bolton.

How soon can you start my building project?

Availability depends on the type and size of the project and our current schedule. Include your preferred timescale with your enquiry so we can discuss what is realistic.
