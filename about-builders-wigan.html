ABOUT L&R DESIGN & BUILD

Experienced Builders in Wigan

L&R Design & Build is a Wigan-based building company with 15 years of construction experience, delivering house extensions, new build homes, brickwork, groundwork, renovations and property improvements across Greater Manchester and the wider North West.

OUR STORY

A Local Building Company Built Through Experience

L&R Design & Build started from a passion for building and construction. The business grew from smaller projects into an established local building company through hard work, customer recommendations and a continued focus on quality workmanship.

The company has now been operating for more than 10 years and brings 15 years of experience within the construction industry. That experience covers residential building work including extensions, new builds, brickwork, structural alterations, groundwork and property renovations.
