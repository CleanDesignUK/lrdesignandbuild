FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About Our Building Services

Find answers about house extensions, new builds, brickwork, groundwork, renovations, project locations and requesting a quote from L&R Design & Build.

What building services do you provide?

We carry out house extensions, new builds, brickwork, groundwork, renovations, garden rooms, garage conversions, landscaping, fencing, driveways, patios, drainage and flagging or paving.

Do you build house extensions?

Yes. We undertake house extensions ranging from smaller additions through to larger single-storey and double-storey extension projects.

Do you take on new build homes?

Yes. Our new build work can cover the construction process from groundwork and foundations through to brickwork and the finished structure.

What brickwork services do you offer?

Our brickwork includes walls, house extensions, repairs, alterations and brickwork for new build projects.

Do you carry out groundwork and drainage?

Yes. Groundwork services include foundations, excavation, ground preparation and drainage work, including installations, repairs and alterations.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Wigan and work across Greater Manchester and the wider North West, including Leigh, Manchester, Salford, Warrington, Wilmslow, Lymm, Macclesfield, Cheshire, Liverpool and Bolton.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes. You can request a free quote by sending us details about the type of work, project location, planning or drawings and your expected timescale.

Do I need planning permission or drawings before contacting you?

You can contact us at any stage. If you already have drawings or planning approval, include those details with your enquiry. If not, let us know the current stage of the project when you get in touch.

Can you help with renovations and structural alterations?

Yes. Renovation work can include structural and internal alterations, creating new openings, removing walls and making changes to improve the layout and use of a property.

What information should I send when requesting a quote?

Tell us what type of work you are considering, where the property is located, whether you have planning permission or drawings and when you would ideally like the work to begin.

Planning a Building Project?

Whether you are considering a house extension, new build, brickwork, groundwork or renovation, tell us about your project and what you would like to achieve.
