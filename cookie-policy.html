1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website can store on your device. Websites may use cookies to remember preferences, maintain functionality or collect information about how a website is used.

Similar technologies, including browser local storage, can also save information on your device. This policy covers both cookies and similar technologies used by this website.

2. How this website uses browser storage

The L&R Design & Build website uses browser storage to remember the choice you make through the cookie consent banner.

This prevents the website from asking you to make the same choice every time you move between pages or return to the website using the same browser.

4. Essential storage

Essential storage supports functionality that is necessary for the website or for services you have requested. For example, the website may remember the cookie choice you have made so that your preference can be respected.

5. Optional analytics

The website's consent controls include an option for analytics.

Optional analytics should not be activated unless you have chosen to allow them. If analytics tools are introduced or changed, this policy should be updated to identify the relevant provider, purpose and storage used.

6. Current analytics implementation

At the time this policy was last updated, the core website does not require optional analytics tracking in order to function. If optional analytics are subsequently enabled on the live website, they should remain disabled until the appropriate consent has been provided.

7. Third-party services

The website uses external services to provide some technical functionality, such as fonts, website libraries and form handling.

When your browser connects to an external service, that provider may receive standard technical information required to deliver the requested resource or service. Any cookies or similar technologies used independently by a third party are subject to that provider's own policies.

8. Website enquiry forms

Website enquiry forms use Web3Forms to transmit the information you enter to L&R Design & Build LTD. Information submitted through a form is covered in more detail in our Privacy Policy .

9. Changing your cookie preferences

You can change your optional cookie preference using the website's cookie controls.

10. Browser controls

Most browsers allow you to view, remove or block cookies and stored website data. The exact steps depend on the browser and device you use.

Removing website data may cause your saved cookie preference to be forgotten, which means the website may ask you to choose your preferences again.

11. Changes to this cookie policy

We may update this policy if the website begins using different cookies, analytics services or similar technologies. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated revision date.
